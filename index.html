About Us :3

Strawberry Jam Cat Cafe is a (fictional) locally owned cat cafe in Lethbridge, Alberta!
We currently have FOUR cats you can visit, play with, and take photos of. And if you really love one (or more) of them and want to give any of these furry critters a loving home, you can even adopt them!
These precious kitties are our pride and joy and we hope you end up loving them as much as we do! Feel free to stop by any time during our opening hours for some furry fun. :3
